# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Brazilian end consumers, primarily 30-60 years old, who care about food provenance and quality. They discover the brand through physical retail in Campos do Jordao or through word of mouth. When visiting the site, they are in a warm, curious mood: they want to confirm the brand is trustworthy, understand the story, browse products, and find where to buy. A secondary segment is gifting buyers choosing something premium for someone they care about.

## Product Purpose

Geleia dos Monges (Fruitland) has produced artisanal jams, sweets, and preserves in the Serra da Mantiqueira since 1983 at 1,600m altitude. The site exists to build brand trust, showcase the product range, communicate the origin story, and convert curious visitors into buyers or contacts. Success means a visitor leaves feeling the brand is worthy of their table.

## Brand Personality

Warm, unhurried, trustworthy. The brand feels like a family recipe handed down with care, not a startup pitch. Tone: affectionate but confident, rooted in place and time. Three words: enraizado, artesanal, afetuoso.

## Anti-references

- Corporate food conglomerates: Nestle, Unilever, Quaker. Sterile, investor-facing design, no soul.
- Mass-retail private label: bright generic packaging, discount-driven, no story.
- Cold minimalism: brands that mistake emptiness for elegance, losing warmth entirely.

## Design Principles

1. Origin is the product. Campos do Jordao, the altitude, the mountain air: these are not decoration, they are the reason the product is good. Lead with place.
2. Time as a value. 1983 is not a trivia fact; it is proof. Design should let age feel earned, not dated.
3. The table is the destination. Every product ultimately ends up on someone's breakfast table. Keep that human warmth present in every touchpoint.
4. Restraint over abundance. Artisanal means fewer, better things. No screen should feel like a supermarket shelf.
5. Trust through texture. Handmade quality communicates through sensory detail: grain, warm color, organic shapes. Never plastic or glossy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text must pass contrast on all background colors used. Reduce-motion preference respected for all Framer Motion animations.
